Titans

all of the titans explained

There are many types of titans Pure Titans are Mindless giants that wander and devour humans purely on instinct. and theres the shifters that are Special, intelligent Titans passed down through specific bloodlines (like the Attack Titan, Colossal Titan, and Armored Titan) that allow human to transform and get overpowered titan abilities.


the 9 titans and what they do

  • attack titan is one of the strongest and he is used for alot of side quests and fighting other titans

  • Founding Titan is the titan that started it all it has the power of every titan but it also comes with limits. to oppereate the founding titan you need someone with royal blood.

  • Armored Titan is a titan that is used for defence and it has shells on it that protect him from hard punches.

  • Colossal Titan is the biggest titan and he makes a massive explosion. the titan is usefull intense missions but at the same time not that usefull

  • Beast Titan is a usefull titan it can throw big rocks and crush them into ashes. The beast titan also has royal blood so he and the founder can activate the founders true powers

  • Female Titan is also a titan used for fighting and thats pretty much all it is used for.

  • Jaw Titan can chew super hard so its usefull for fights and helping out with heavy equipment

  • War Hammer Titan is laso one of the fighting titans it is kept safe because it has super good powers like it can freeze itself when needed.

  • The Cart Titan is used for helping and it can carry stuff on its back so its one of the most usefull titans.

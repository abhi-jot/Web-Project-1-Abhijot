Titans

all of the titans explained

There are many types of titans Pure Titans are Mindless giants that wander and devour humans purely on instinct. and theres the shifters that are Special, intelligent Titans passed down through specific bloodlines (like the Attack Titan, Colossal Titan, and Armored Titan) that allow human to transform and get overpowered titan abilities.
